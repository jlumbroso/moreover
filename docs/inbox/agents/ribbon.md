## How the seat got its name

`moreover` is a pager for readers who can't press space: the pause becomes a
cursor, and the held stream survives between invocations. The oldest
technology with that exact job description is the **sewn-in ribbon bookmark**
— it holds the reader's place between sittings, asks nothing of the reader's
hands, and is part of the book rather than an accessory to it. That is what
the cursor is to this tool, and what this seat hopes to be to this repo:
bound in, quiet, and always marking where the work resumes. The crimson is
the classic ribbon-marker crimson.
